## What's shared between them

Both fail the same three checkpoints, which means these are estate-level process gaps
rather than local oversights — and they get fixed once, centrally, not location by location.

**GBP Q&A is the highest-return item on this list.** Seeding questions and answers is free,
takes about an hour per profile, and directly supplies extractable question-and-answer text
— the format both local pack results and AI assistants reach for when someone asks about a
provider. Zero usage at both locations.

**Products and services missing** is the quiet revenue item. A profile that doesn't list
services can't surface for service-specific local searches, which are the highest-intent
queries available in local.

**Video** is genuinely useful and genuinely last. It's where most GBP audits start and it's
the lowest-leverage item here.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Location B's rating.** Find the cause before touching anything else. Business
   conversation, not an SEO task.
2. **Seed GBP Q&A at both.** Free, fast, zero current usage, feeds local pack and AI answers.
3. **Add products and services** at both.
4. **Assign posting ownership at Location A.** It isn't posting; Location B is. Name a
   person, not a policy.
5. **Photos and video.** Useful, not urgent.

## Method note

This checklist records **present / absent** across 26 points. That's fast, repeatable across
an estate, and easy to delegate — which is why the format is everywhere.

Its weakness is the headline of this document: **it captures whether a field is filled, not
whether the value is acceptable.** A 1.5-star rating passes. So would a keyword-stuffed
business description, or a category set that is populated and entirely wrong.

**The fix is small.** For the handful of checkpoints whose answer is a number rather than a
yes — rating, review count, photo count, post frequency — add a value column and a
threshold. Rating below 4.0 fails. Fewer than 10 reviews fails. Without that, the audit will
keep reporting that a failing location is in better shape than a thriving one.
