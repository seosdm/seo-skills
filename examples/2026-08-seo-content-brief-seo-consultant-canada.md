**Business goal:** convert (lead gen), not traffic volume

---

## Recommendation

**Update the existing page. Do not build a new one.**

`/seo-consultant-canada.html` already targets this term and is already well-optimised
where it's cheap to be — the title tag is `SEO Consultant Canada | Semil Shah — Toronto,
Ontario` and the meta description carries the proof points. The problem isn't targeting,
it's substance: **420 words of body copy** (measured from source, nav/footer/scripts
stripped) against a ranking set running to several thousand. A second page would
cannibalise this one and inherit the same thinness.

One caveat stated up front, because it changes how you should value this work: on this
SERP, content depth is not the primary ranking lever — reviews and entity authority are.
See *What would make this fail*. This brief is worth executing, but it is not sufficient
on its own.

## Target & intent

**Primary:** SEO consultant Canada
**Secondaries:** SEO consultant Toronto · SEO expert Canada · hire SEO consultant Canada ·
freelance SEO consultant Canada

**Intent: commercial investigation.** Nobody searching this is looking to learn what SEO
is. They are building a shortlist and want to know who is credible, what it costs, and
what happens if they book a call.

**SERP format to match:** the SERP is **mixed and directory-dominated**. Of the results
sampled across two queries, roughly six of ten page-one slots go to directories and
marketplaces — Clutch, Upwork, Toptal, Truelancer, freel.ca, unicornfactory.ca — plus a
listicle. Individual consultant pages hold only a minority of slots.

**This is the single most important fact in the brief.** You are not competing for ten
positions; you are competing for three or four. The realistic goal is to be the best
*individual consultant* result, and separately to be listed inside the directories that
own the rest of the page. The second half of that is not a content problem and is out of
scope here.

**AI Overview:** not verified for this query — it needs a manual logged-out check. Given
the commercial intent and directory dominance, expect either no AI Overview or one that
synthesises the directory listings. If the latter, the extractable win is a crisp,
factual "who is X and what do they do" block near the top of the page.

## Angle

**The consultant who publishes his actual methods, in public, for free.**

Not "more comprehensive." Semil has seven public GitHub repos containing the real skills
he runs on client work — crawl analysis, GSC opportunity mining, AI visibility auditing —
with worked examples showing the output on real data. Nobody else on this SERP has
anything comparable, and it's independently verifiable in one click.

This converts the weakest part of the current page into the strongest. Right now it says
"400% organic growth" for an unnamed client, which reads like every other consultant
page. "Here is the exact audit methodology, here is what it produced on a real site, go
read the code" is a claim competitors can't copy without doing the work.

It is also the honest answer to the buyer's real question — *will this person actually
know what they're doing, or am I buying a deck?*

## Audience & stage

A Canadian founder, marketing lead, or agency owner who has decided they need outside SEO
help and is assembling a shortlist of three. They have probably already opened a Clutch
listing and one or two consultant sites in other tabs.

They leave the page to either book a call or dismiss you. There is no third outcome, and
nothing on this page should be optimised for anything else.

## Outline

**H1: SEO Consultant in Canada**

The current H1 is *"SEO That Wins In Canada & The U.S."* — it does not contain the target
keyword. Change it. The keyword should appear in the H1 verbatim, with the positioning
moving to the subhead beneath it.

**→ Direct answer block (above the fold, immediately under H1)**

*This is the section that gets extracted into AI answers and featured snippets. Write it
first.* Three to four sentences, plain and factual: who Semil is, where he's based, how
long he's worked, who he works with, and what engagement models exist. No adjectives. If
a model had to answer "who is Semil Shah" from this page alone, this block is what it
would use.

**H2: What a Canadian SEO consultant actually does**
Scope of the engagement in concrete terms. Distinguish consultant from agency from
in-house hire — this is the comparison the reader is silently running.

**H2: Proof — what the work has produced**
The current "Track Record" section, rebuilt. Each result needs client type, starting
position, timeframe, and metric. "400% organic growth" alone is not proof; "400% organic
growth for a B2B SaaS over 14 months, from 1.2k to 6k monthly sessions" is. If clients
can't be named, name the vertical and the shape of the engagement.

**H2: How I work — and the methods are public**
The differentiator section. Link the GitHub repos. Show one real worked example. This is
the section no competitor on this SERP can replicate.

**H2: Services**
Keep the existing H3 structure — Fractional SEO Manager, Technical SEO Audits, Content
Marketing & SEO, Local SEO, eCommerce SEO. Each H3 currently has almost no body copy;
give each 60–90 words and an internal link to its own page.

**H2: AI search visibility — GEO and AEO**
Currently missing entirely, and a direct competitor is claiming this ground in his title
tag. Cover what changes when answers are generated rather than ranked, and what an
audit measures. Link the GEO framework post and `/brand-visibility.html`.

**H2: What it costs**
**Add this — the page currently has no pricing at all.** Both ranking individual
consultants publish theirs ($1,000/mo and $300/mo respectively). You do not need an exact
number; a range, a starting point, or an explicit "engagements typically start at X"
removes the biggest friction on a commercial-intent page. Silence on price reads as
expensive.

**H2: Canada and the U.S. — how coverage works**
Short. Toronto/Ontario base, remote across Canada and the U.S., timezone overlap. Local
signal without a fake office-locations section.

**H2: Frequently asked questions**
8–10 questions, from the list below. Mark up with FAQPage schema.

**H2: Book a consultation**
Single CTA. The current page has two competing CTAs; pick one.

## Must-cover entities & subtopics

Present across the ranking set and absent or thin on the current page:

- **Engagement models** — fractional, retainer, project audit, coaching. Named and priced.
- **Pricing anchors** — a real number or range

Deliberately excluded: "keyword research", "on-page SEO", "backlinks" as standalone
explainers. Commercial-intent readers don't need them defined, and they pad the page
toward Anand's format without Anand's authority.

## What would make this fail

**The honest risk: this SERP is won on authority signals, not content depth.** Paul
Teitelman ranks with 101 Google reviews and logos from the Globe and Mail and Rogers.
Semil's page displays no review count and no named clients. A well-executed 2,000-word
page will not out-rank that on content quality alone, and anyone promising otherwise is
selling you something.

**The realistic outcome** is a materially better page that converts the traffic it already
gets and becomes competitive *once* review volume and named references exist. Treat the
content work as necessary but not sufficient — and run the review-generation and Clutch
listing work in parallel, because that is the actual constraint.

**Second risk: the AI positioning is contested.** A competitor already ranks with GEO/AEO
in his title tag. Semil's version has to be demonstrably deeper — the published repos and
real worked examples are how that's won, not by claiming the same words harder.
